case briefing · 2016-0315

A murder mystery across ten years: question the past by radio, catch the lies, name the killer.

March 15, 2016, 23:23. Julia Han, co-founder and CTO of Moonlight Labs, is found dead in the server room, the night before the startup signs its first big investment. The police call it an electric shock accident and close the case. Ten years later you are a cold-case detective. In the evidence box you find an old radio, and at 23:23 it turns on. Julia answers, 33 minutes before she dies.
